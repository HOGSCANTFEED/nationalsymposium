Common questions

Anyone doing paediatric feeding work — whether that's your whole role in a multidisciplinary clinic, or part of a broader caseload in a community team, regional service or private practice.

Medicine, speech pathology, dietetics, occupational therapy, psychology, nursing and allied health all sit in the same room. If you are a clinician interested in children with feeding difficulties, you belong here.

Speakers share practical experiences, resources and innovations from real clinics. It isn't a research forum and makes no claims to best practice.

Not everything you hear will fit your setting — take what's useful and leave the rest. A practical idea you can apply this week often beats a paper stuck in a fifteen-year knowledge-translation gap.

Sessions run live and aren't recorded or shared afterwards. That's deliberate: it keeps the room a safe, collegial space where people can talk openly about what actually happens in their clinic.

Cameras and microphones stay off for attendees; questions come through the chat.

National Paediatric Feeding Symposium

Convened by Chris, Frances and Heidi as part of the education role in the District-wide feeding team.

A clinician-led information and practice-sharing forum. It is not a research event and makes no claims to endrose best practice.

This is not a clinical service. We can't take appointments, referrals or clinical enquiries here. If you're a parent or carer looking for help with your child's feeding, please speak to your GP, paediatrician or treating team.

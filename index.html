What it is — and what it isn't

An ideas-sharing forum for clinicians

Paediatric feeding is inherently multidisciplinary and, in many services, isolating. This is a space to hear a good idea you can use tomorrow — because a practical idea you can apply today is often more powerful than a paper stuck in a 15-year knowledge-translation gap.

A collegial space to share what works

Practical, positive experiences, resources and innovations from real clinics — across medicine, speech pathology, dietetics, occupational therapy, psychology, nursing and allied health.

Not a research forum

This is not a research presentation and makes no claims to best practice. Not everything you hear will apply to your setting — take what's useful, leave the rest.

Clinician-only & live

Registrations are reviewed so the room stays a safe, collegial space for clinicians. Sessions are live only and are not recorded or made available afterwards.

Free, online, over lunch

A 90-minute lunchtime session on Microsoft Teams, run about four times a year. Cameras and microphones stay off; questions come through the chat.

Who it's for. Anyone doing paediatric feeding work — whether it's your whole role in a multidisciplinary clinic, or part of a broader caseload in a community team, regional service or private practice. If you support children with feeding difficulties, you belong here.
